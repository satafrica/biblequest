## r1 fixes

- wis-w9-5: The scenario has the person say God put the wallet there 'to test me.' Scripture does affirm that God tests people (James 1:2-3, 12; Genesis 22:1). So the error James 1:13 actually addresses is blaming God for enticement to evil, not the idea of being tested. Rewording to 'tempt me' keeps the scenario aligned with the cited verse and avoids implying that believing God tests people is wrong. Otherwise the marked answer is accurate and the distractors are clearly contrary.
- wis-w12-6: The hint slightly misreads the verse. In Mark 6:31, 'many coming and going' describes the crowds, not the disciples. Jesus' invitation was to the disciples, who 'had no leisure so much as to eat.' Everything else is accurate, and the wrong options are clearly contrary to the verse.
- wis-w12-4: The scenario has the relative listening to a guest's ordinary stories. In the passage, the 'good part' Mary chose was hearing Jesus' word, so equating it with listening to a guest's stories distorts what is 'needful'. The explanation's phrase 'sitting and hearing his word' comes from v39, which is outside the cited text. Option B ('Ask the guest to step in…') is worded awkwardly.
- wis-w12-8: The hint ('bird imagery') gives the answer away, because only the marked option mentions wings or flying. That makes the item trivial despite its difficulty rating of 4. Otherwise the item is sound: the marked answer paraphrases the KJV directly, and each distractor contradicts the passage. Option B contradicts 'cease from thine own wisdom', option A contradicts 'set thine eyes upon that which is not', and option C contradicts 'labour not to be rich'. The scenario is a moral one rather than financial advice.
- mys-m13-2: The clue adds a detail Scripture does not give: it says 'one of the travellers held up a piece of dry bread.' In Joshua 9:12 the speech is collective ('This our bread we took hot...'), and no single speaker or gesture is described. Everything else is accurate to the KJV: the briefing's details match 9:4-6, and the answer, explanation and hint fit the text.
- mys-m17-5: The clue's phrase 'the prophet said his heart had gone with him' is ambiguous and could be read as Gehazi's heart. In the KJV, Elisha says 'Went not mine heart with thee', meaning Elisha's own heart went with Gehazi. The explanation has the same ambiguity.
- mys-m17-4: The clue says 'near the hill', but the KJV says 'the tower', so it contradicts the supplied text. 'The goods were not seen again' adds a detail Scripture does not give. The question, options, answer, explanation and hint are sound.
- mys-m14-1: The hint gives the answer away: 'what kind of garden he named' points to the only option containing the word 'garden.' Otherwise the clue, prompt, answer and explanation match 1 Kings 21:1-2.
- mys-m18-2: The clue adds an unscriptural detail: 'A later inspection noted marks on the stone.' Matthew records no inspection and no marks on the stone. The question, options, answer, explanation and hint are otherwise accurate to Matthew 27:65-66.
- mys-m18-3: The clue adds a detail Scripture does not give. 'Witnesses felt the ground shake' implies particular people, possibly the women, experienced the earthquake. The text says only 'there was a great earthquake' and does not say who felt it. The hint is somewhat revealing but acceptable.
- mys-m20-1: The clue says 'small silver shrines,' but the KJV says only 'silver shrines'; 'small' is an added detail. 'Craftsmen of the city' is a slight embellishment of 'the craftsmen.' Everything else is accurate, and no distractor is defensible: Alexander and Gaius appear in the chapter but did not call the craftsmen together.
- mys-m20-3: The briefing already says the crowd 'packed the theatre', and only one option mentions the theatre. That gives away the answer without the player needing the names. The hint ('adventure himself' into this place) also points only to the location. The clue and the answer are otherwise accurate to Acts 19:29-31.
- par-p19-4: The explanation says the fig tree was 'given one more year', but the text records only the dresser's request ('let it alone this year also'). The owner's answer is never stated. The rest of the item is accurate.
- par-p14-4: The hint says the householder 'shares' his treasure, which interprets beyond the KJV wording. The text says only that he 'bringeth forth out of his treasure things new and old'. Everything else is accurate.
- par-p20-3: The explanation says 'After filling his house with others', but the text only records the lord ordering the servant to compel guests in 'that my house may be filled'. The filling itself is not narrated. Also, the speaker of v.24 ('For I say unto you', plural) is ambiguous: it could be the lord or Jesus addressing his hearers. Attributing it firmly to the master in the prompt is slightly interpretive, though it is a common reading.
- par-p22-1: The hint ('ten times the size') fits options B (1000/100) and D (10/1) as well as A, so it does not help the player narrow the choices. It is not inaccurate, just ineffective. Everything else is accurate to Luke 7:36-41.
